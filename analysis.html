Worth taking

Defuse the phone field in the placeholder

“for text reminders — we will NEVER call you.” Our opt-in asks for a number and says nothing. Six words against a measurable drop-off, and it costs nothing to test.

Build in a second close

His bonus Day 6 exists purely to re-close non-buyers, and it runs 139 minutes — not a token follow-up.

Split the payment decision

A small deposit, a signature, then the balance. Commitment escalates in three steps instead of one. Directly applicable to our own checkout.

Teach something certifiable

Day 2 is a real QuickBooks skill with a certification attached. It converts “free challenge” into “I already got something of value” before any money is asked for.

Read carefully

Whisper renders his prices without the thousands separator — $4,997 appears as “49.97” and $1,997 as “1997”. Worse, “1997” is also a value-stack figure in the same pitch (“those coaching calls are a 1997 value”), so the same token means two different things. The price here is verified because it is stated three separate times — Day 5 at 01:52:45 and 02:02:01, and Day 6 at 01:21:29 — and all three agree.
